# İSPATLA brand standard

The displayed product name is **ispatla.tr**, paired with the supplied site mark in visual brand labels. Do not display the legacy uppercase ISPATLA/İSPATLA name. Plain-text titles and metadata use ispatla.tr.

The exact Turkish slogan is **Algoritmaya tapma. İspatla.** Preserve its punctuation. The English rendering is **Don’t worship the algorithm. Prove it.** Every landing locale preserves this challenge-and-proof meaning in its headline; keep product explanation in the intro.

## Product message

𝕏 sources → account relevance and reasoning → editable AI draft → user decision → observed outcome with its evidence level. Multiple accounts and optional individual/bulk review are supported. GitHub is the code and contribution channel, not a content-source network.

Lead with the slogan, one useful sentence, a direct action and the interactive workspace. Give marketing room to work. Avoid generic AI-workspace badges, “demo example” banners, bureaucratic warnings, feature walls and repetitive disclaimers. Detailed setup/method boundaries go in help or optional disclosure. Do not imply invented posts, measured engagement or customer results are live facts.

## Assets and design

- Use supplied `public/brand/logo.svg` and `public/brand/brain.svg`. AI actions use the brain, not sparkle or robot icons. Preserve the wordmark in Libron.
- Archive Paper `#F0EDE5`, Carbon Ink `#16191E`, Signal Cobalt `#315BF5`, Warm Archive `#9A9A91`.
- Rejected `#BC453D` and Verified `#477865` need text labels when meaning depends on them.
- Use editorial hierarchy, spacing and readable contrast. No decorative glow, glass, animated branding gradients, random symbols or 3D spheres.
- The separator in marketing, titles and metadata is **⁜**. Do not use a middle dot or em dash as the brand separator.
- The platform mark is exactly **𝕏** (U+1D54F), as specified by AGENTS.md.
- User-handle placeholders use **@elonmusk**. Do not attribute invented content or performance to that person.

## Voice

Challenge unsupported claims; never belittle the user. Product steps are short and calm. Safety/help content is professional. Plain verbs are better than vague promises about impact, acceleration or transformation.

Open source means inspectable AGPL-3.0-or-later code. It does not mean zero AI/API/server cost or automatic safety. No viral guarantees, invented results, fake testimonials, fake scarcity or unapproved prices/Prime features.

English is the default site language on bare routes, regardless of browser language. Explicit locale paths and saved choices remain supported; Turkish uses `/tr`.

Keep all 20 locales complete. Translate product meaning naturally and preserve RTL. Never add a competitor section or resurrect old campaign art simply because a file exists.

Current strategy, research limits, campaign drafts, experiments and open decisions: [docs/PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md](docs/PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md).

Shared shape standard: 4px small details, 6px compact elements, 8px controls, 12px surfaces, 16px outer frames. Use circles for intrinsically circular elements such as avatars. Layout spacing follows the existing 4px unit; nearby content uses 8–12px, related groups 24px and sections 48–80px responsively. Research rationale and limits: [docs/SHAPE_AND_SPACING.md](docs/SHAPE_AND_SPACING.md).

The shared BrandLogo uses a 40px mark / 24px wordmark on mobile and 48px / 28px from the small breakpoint upward. Navigation must not override these dimensions per page; wrap the header contents when needed.

All shared navigation/footer wordmarks use public/brand/wordmark.svg, outlined from the supplied Libron Bold with tight visible-ink bounds. CSS font-line-box offsets are not used. Shared page starts use site-container; reading forms keep their maximum width without recentering away from the header.
