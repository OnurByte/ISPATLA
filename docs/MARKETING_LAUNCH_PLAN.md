## Publication

Choose campaign/channel; verify the claim and destination in the current build; review source date, context, crop and alt text. Real results need permission and complete evidence. Failed/unknown outcomes cannot become wins. No reply/DM spam, engagement exchange or forced-follow gate.

## Measurement

E1–E7 in the strategy specify hypotheses, tasks, metrics and limits. Daily events and retained signup/draft timing are separate snapshots; event ratios are not unique-user conversion or causal evidence. Human interviews/experiments have not run merely because a protocol exists.

Suggested sequencing: days 1–15 understanding/claim audit, 16–30 selected content tests, 31–60 first-value friction, 61–90 measured learning. These are future windows, not elapsed time or acquired traffic.

Live deployment, human recruitment, social publication, native-language review, Prime pricing and permission for public cards remain explicit external/product decisions. Source/local checks are in [BRIEF_ACCEPTANCE.md](BRIEF_ACCEPTANCE.md).
