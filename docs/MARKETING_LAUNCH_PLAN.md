# The Signal Press launch plan

## Message order

1. Open source and user control.
2. Research, opportunity review and evidence tracking.
3. Build an adaptable workflow.
4. Compare tools using dated, checkable evidence.

Approved positioning: **Büyümeyi vaat edenler var. Biz nasıl çalıştığımızı
gösteriyoruz.** Supporting line: **Akışı herkes görür. Sinyali sen yakala.**
Creative sign-off: **THE SIGNAL PRESS — Open code. Visible decisions. Verifiable
outcomes.** Never imply guaranteed growth or viral outcomes.

## Campaigns

- **Anti-Growth Guarantee:** “GUARANTEED VIRALITY” resolves to “Nobody can promise
  that. We can show you the signal, the decision and the evidence.” Do not imply
  other services are fraudulent.
- **Don't Trust Us:** pair “DON'T TRUST ISPATLA.” with “Read the code.” and links to
  actual source, license, product state and architecture.
- **Receipt Challenge:** “Claim it? Show the receipt.” Only link evidence for
  confirmed product behavior; invite voluntary, authentic examples.
- **Open Product Autopsy:** publish failures and corrections from synthetic or
  consented examples; remove sensitive information.
- **Open Source Switch:** show real repository paths and accessible route back to
  product/signup.
- **Developer Field Notes:** state what changed, why, which checks ran, what failed
  and what remains. Never present planned work as shipped.
- **Public Comparison Challenge:** accept source-based corrections and show dated
  revisions.
- **Hidden Archive:** optional design notes with no follow/repost gate or reward
  exchange.

No fake users, artificial engagement pools, unsolicited automated messages or
organized spam. No publishing or external posting is performed by this plan.

## Release gates

Confirm live routes, repo/license links and demo availability; review every factual
claim against its source and observation date; make social art legible at platform
crop sizes; add accessible alt text; verify campaign links; and label synthetic
examples. Keep product build, browser behavior, production availability, analytics
and social posting as separate evidence categories.
